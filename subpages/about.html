About me

Software engineering student who likes the parts of an app you never see: the services, queues and pipelines underneath.

I like the backend because that's where the real decisions live: how data flows, how services talk to each other, and what happens when something fails.

I'm Arne, a third-year Applied Computer Science student at Howest, majoring in Software Engineering. I started out studying Computer Science at Ghent University before moving to Howest.

Next to my studies I work part-time at Sempl, where I build features for a smart pool controller across the Go backend and the Flutter app.

I graduate in June 2027. Have a project, an internship or a bug to squash? Let's talk.
